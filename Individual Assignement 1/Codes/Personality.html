My Personality Traits

Due to my current career position i am always on the go. Stressing, however i have to try my best to pull my weight and get whatever task done as efficiently as possible.

First Personality Trait

One of my strongest personality traits is determination. I believe that once I set a goal for myself, I should make every effort to achieve it, even when faced with challenges. This is reflected in my decision to pursue a Bachelor of Science in Computer Networking and Security at the University of Technology, Jamaica, while also managing my work and family responsibilities.

Second Personality Trait

Another personality trait that I believe describes me is analytical thinking. Because of the nature of my work in digital forensics, I have developed the habit of examining information carefully before arriving at a conclusion. I prefer to understand how something works and why a particular problem occurs rather than simply accepting an answer without questioning it.

Third Personality Trait

My third personality trait that I believe describes me is adaptability. I have learned that things do not always go according to plan, and sometimes adjustments have to be made to achieve a particular goal. Working in law enforcement has exposed me to different situations where I have had to adjust quickly, learn new things and find ways to complete my responsibilities despite challenges.

My Views on AI in Education

LLMs Must Be Used as a Tool, Not a Crutch

As a university student, I believe that artificial intelligence is very useful in education, but it depends on how students use it. When I hear the statement that LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot should be used as a tool and not a crutch, my understanding is that these applications should assist with learning rather than replace the effort students are expected. The main purpose of attending university is to gain knowledge and develop skills that can be used in the real world. Strong dependency on AI to complete every task without understanding the information, well I might receive a passing grade but still lack the knowledge.

How AI Can Support My Learning

One of the main ways AI can assist me is by explaining topics that I find difficult to understand. being that i am pursuing a degree in Computer Networking and Security, there are times when I encounter technical concepts that require additional explanation. AI is useful in these situations because I can ask questions and request simpler explanations or examples. This help me understand information that I may not fully understand from a lecture or textbook. Another benefit is that AI can assist with troubleshooting. For example, when working on programming or networking assignments, I can use AI to help identify possible reasons why something is not working. However, I believe it is important for me to understand the correction rather than simply copying the solution.

The Risks of Depending Too Much on AI

AI has several advantages, I believe depending on it too much can negatively affect a student's development. One major concern is that students may become accustomed to receiving answers without taking the time to research or think critically about the problem. AI can also provide information that is incorrect or misleading. If a student accepts everything generated by AI without checking it against reliable sources, that student may end up learning incorrect information or submitting inaccurate assignments.

My Approach to Responsible AI Use

My approach to using AI in education is to treat it as an additional learning resource. I believe students should first make an effort to understand their coursework and attempt assignments on their own before seeking assistance. When using AI, I believe it is important to review the information provided, compare it with course materials and other reliable sources, and ensure that I understand the final answer.
